Welcome to My Needlepoint Corner!

Needlepoint has become my favorite hobby because sometimes you just need to put the phone down, pick up a needle, and stitch your problems away!

This project is a simple webpage created to share my love of needlepointing. I wanted to create something that was informative but also showed some personality. After all, life is too short for boring hobbies!

🪡 Why Needlepoint?

In a world where we spend so much time scrolling, multitasking, and feeling overstimulated, needlepoint gives us a chance to pause for a little while. Needlepoint is a creative, cozy, and relaxing hobby that allows me to do just that

I enjoy choosing different canvas designs, picking out thread colors, and watching a project come together one stitch at a time.

It's a great excuse to sit down, relax, and say "just one more stitch."

...and then realize an hour has passed.

✂️ What's in My Needlepoint Bag?

A successful needlepoint project requires a few important supplies:

  • 🧵 Thread
  • 🪡 Needles
  • 🎨 Needlepoint canvas
  • ✂️ Scissors
  • 📌 Needle minder

💡 Beginner Tips

If you're thinking about giving needlepoint a try, but don't know where to begin, here are a few tips that helped me:

  1. Start with a design you actually like.
  2. Don't worry about making every stitch perfect. The back of your canvas is none of your business!
  3. Keep your thread organized.
  4. Take your time. It's meant to be a relaxing hobby.
  5. Remember - mistakes can usually be fixed.
  6. Most importantly, HAVE FUN!
